01 / The opportunity

A real operator, not a rented license.

NITERUN Insurance Network is being established as an independent insurance agency operation. The founding DRLP would help create and supervise the procedures needed for lawful, high-quality insurance distribution.

We're looking for a professional who takes regulatory obligations seriously, understands agency economics, and can build an initial licensed producer team with clear responsibility and real operational authority.

Leadership and agency ownership are distinct: the DRLP role does not require buying equity in the business.

02 / Responsibilities

Where your experience matters.

  • Actively oversee licensing and compliance responsibilities required of the designated producer.
  • Guide agency licensing, carrier/IMO agreements, E&O coverage, and producer onboarding.
  • Establish practical supervision, marketing review, quality control, and escalation processes.
  • Help evaluate and support an initial team of properly licensed insurance producers.
  • Optionally write insurance business personally where appropriately licensed and appointed.
03 / Candidate profile

What we're looking for.

Active NC producer license

Appropriate individual North Carolina insurance producer authority; life-insurance experience is especially valuable for the planned initial launch.

Meaningful operational experience

Agency management, producer supervision, carrier relationships, regulatory processes, or a prior DRLP role. Five or more years preferred.

Real availability and judgment

Ability to supervise the work, act ethically, respond when needed, and work within a transparent and properly documented arrangement.

04 / Partnership structure

Build with aligned incentives.

We are exploring a flexible, lean leadership agreement with negotiated compensation for actual supervisory responsibilities and eligible insurance production. No salary, specific commission percentage, lead volume, or earnings amount is guaranteed.

Any employment or independent-contractor classification, compensation terms, and regulator or carrier requirements will be evaluated before an agreement is signed. We are not seeking someone who only lends their license.
